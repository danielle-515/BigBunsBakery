About us!

Hello! We are Big Buns Bakery, a family-owned bakery dedicated to providing delicious baked goods made from scratch with the finest ingredients. Our passion for baking started generations ago, and we take pride in sharing our love for baked treats with our community.

Allergens Information

At Big Buns Bakery, we understand the importance of allergen information for our customers. We take great care in preparing our baked goods and strive to provide accurate information regarding potential allergens present in our products. Common allergens such as nuts, dairy, gluten, and eggs may be present in some of our items. We recommend that customers with specific dietary restrictions or allergies inform our staff when placing an order so we can assist in selecting suitable options. Your safety and satisfaction are our top priorities!
