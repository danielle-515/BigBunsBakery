Our Story

Welcome to Big Buns Bakery, Purdue University’s student-run bakery where passion, creativity, and community come together — one sweet bite at a time!

Founded and operated entirely by Purdue students, our bakery is more than just a place to grab a treat — it’s a space where learning meets flavor. Every cookie, loaf, and pastry is crafted with care by students who are combining hands-on experience with their love for baking and entrepreneurship.

From classic chocolate chip cookies to innovative seasonal creations, we aim to make every product a reflection of the Boiler spirit: bold, hardworking, and full of heart. Whether you’re fueling up for class, celebrating after exams, or just craving something homemade, BB Bakeryr is here to brighten your day.

We’re proud to serve our Purdue community — by students, for students. 💛🖤

History

It all started in 2021, when a few friends in Earhart Hall realized they were better at rising dough than Purdue’s football team was at rising in the rankings. Armed with a single hand mixer, an illegal toaster oven, and an overconfidence usually reserved for engineering majors, they started baking late at night just to survive the semester.

Word spread fast — first among floormates, then across campus. Soon, students were lining up in hallways to snag warm buns and cookies between classes. What began as a midnight snack operation quickly turned into a full-blown campus craze.

By 2022, the team had partnered with the Purdue Foundry and a few supportive faculty mentors to turn their baking hobby into a real student business. They secured a small kitchen, printed their first aprons (with “We Knead Dough” across the front, obviously), and officially launched Big Buns Bakery.

Today, BB Bakery stands as a proud Boilermaker success story — proof that with enough sugar, caffeine, and good humor, students can build something incredible. We’re still fueled by the same mix of chaos, friendship, and community that started it all.

Our motto? If the score’s low on the field, it’s high in flavor here. 💛🖤
